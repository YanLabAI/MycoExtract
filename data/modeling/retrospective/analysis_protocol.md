# Expanded external retrospective check

Search date 2026-10-25. Scope broadened to mycotoxin-converting enzymes published in 2023–2026, prioritizing 2025–2026 and admitting older studies absent from original training. This is a targeted search with full-text eligibility checks, not an exhaustive systematic review.

Freeze the original feature definitions, training records, StandardScaler and Ridge alpha=10. No candidate-driven model or hyperparameter selection. Include all retrievable measured enzymes in each selected paper, not just favorable predictions. Do not infer kcat from specific activity or Vmax. Do not create a reported efficiency by dividing other reported parameters. Native proteins and explicit mutants may be scored; immobilized systems and unresolved constructs are separated. Exact training-sequence matches are excluded from unseen-sequence rankings. Reference-sequence proxies are reported separately.

Before executing the expanded predictions, use mean absolute log10 error for kcat and Km jointly to rank complete two-target enzyme cases, and the corresponding paper-level mean over all such enzymes to rank whole studies. Report individual target errors and training-mean baseline. At least three measured enzymes with both targets are required to describe a multi-enzyme group. Keep one-target cases and independently regressed efficiency as separate results; no selection of whichever target looks best. A geometric error factor is 10 to the mean absolute log10 error; it is not a percent accuracy. A best observed case/group is selected after evaluation and cannot estimate generalization performance. Predictions do not prove catalytic function, detoxification, or prospective discovery.
